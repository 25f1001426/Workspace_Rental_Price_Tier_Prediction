#3. For numerical features, median imputation is used for columns such as operator_platform_age_years, operator_platform_age_months, operator_active_years, operator_active_months, and operator_portfolio_size because they have very low missingness and may contain skewed distributions. The median is also used for utility_rooms, zones, and workstations, as these variables represent numerical counts and may contain extreme values. For utility_rooms and zones, which have relatively higher proportions of missing data, a missingness indicator is also considered to determine whether the absence of information provides additional predictive value. Similarly, the booking-term columns with a small number of missing values are imputed using the median after investigating potential anomalies and extreme values. For the rating-related columns, including score_overall, score_accuracy, score_condition, score_onboarding, score_support, score_access, and score_value, median imputation is used because it is robust to skewness and helps preserve observations. A missingness indicator is also considered for these columns and reviews_per_month, since missing review information may be associated with workspaces that have no reviews or incomplete profile information. However, the usefulness of missingness indicators is evaluated through model validation rather than assumed in advance.
For categorical features, the missing values in operator_ref are replaced with a separate category labelled "Missing" if the feature is used in subsequent analysis. However, it is excluded from the initial baseline model because of its high cardinality. The missing values in operator_id_verified, operator_has_avatar, and operator_certified are also assigned a "Missing" category to preserve the distinction between unknown information and explicitly recorded values. For borough, missing values are assigned to a separate "Missing" category because a substantial proportion of the data is unavailable, and the observed values may still provide useful predictive information. Similarly, the missing values in bookable are treated as a separate "Missing" category to distinguish unknown status from explicitly unbookable workspaces. Finally, categorical columns with no missing values do not require imputation or additional transformations, thereby avoiding unnecessary preprocessing.
